# I-95 (VA) visualization portal — attribution

This portal is a **self-contained visualization testbed** for MapMatching4GMNS: open
[`datahub.html`](datahub.html) in a browser (deck.gl), or load [`gmns.kml`](gmns.kml) in Google
Earth, or the `*.kepler.json` configs in kepler.gl. The GMNS geometry is in
[`network.geojson`](network.geojson).

## Provenance

The underlying I-95 data was produced with the **USDOT JPO CodeHub "Data Cleaning and Fusion
Tool"**, released by FHWA under the **MIT License**:

- Repository: <https://github.com/usdot-jpo-codehub/data-cleaning-and-fusion-tool>
- License: MIT (see that repository's `LICENSE`)

Per that project's documentation, the bundled datasets are **synthesized** demonstration datasets
distributed through the ITS DOT data portal — not raw proprietary probe records. The visualization
artifacts here (`datahub.html`, `gmns.kml`, kepler/deck.gl configs) were generated by
**GUI4GMNS** from that synthesized I-95 output.

`SOURCE.txt` records the original provenance label (the tool ingests INRIX/RITIS/VDOT feed
*types*; that label describes the tool, not a redistribution restriction on this synthesized
demo output).

## Using it with the adapter

```python
from mapmatching4gmns.adapters import i95
i95.portal_to_network("examples/portals/i95_va", "out/i95_network")
# -> out/i95_network/node.csv, link.csv  (GMNS, from network.geojson)
```

The sanitized synthetic CI fixture lives separately in `examples/self_demo/01_i95/`; this portal
is the richer visualization companion.
